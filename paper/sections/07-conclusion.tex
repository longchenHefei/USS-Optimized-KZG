\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

KHVC reduces the USS reference string to linear size, supports closed-form one-hot updates, and retains position binding together with position hiding in CGLT23's exact Figure~6 view. Theorem~\ref{thm:literal-one-hop} shows that the literal CGLT23 one-hop confidentiality experiments are unattainable by every correct publicly updatable USS, so security cannot honestly be claimed in that literal model. We therefore use transitive exposure closure, complete direct-disclosure bookkeeping, and public-length challenge domains. The concrete payload construction still uses independent encrypted Ring-LWR/RLWE file masks and refreshes a private client offset during KeyUp; RISE remains confined to constant-size openings. Theorem~\ref{thm:adaptive-full} proves IND-DD-UP, IND-FileUp-CPA, and IND-REENC-CPA in this repaired full adaptive game, including disconnected key exposure and delayed transcripts, without a suffix restriction. Its shadow-header construction removes the invalid direct two-sample Ring-LWE shortcut, and its body and opening reductions cover adaptive FileUp continuation. The joint wide-noise phase-residual lemma supplies the real-key boundary simulation, while the $N_H=16384$ parameter budget preserves 127 rotations per file version at a cost of 1 MiB per header and a 65.004 MiB shared token. Fresh FileUp opening blinding blocks the earlier source-comparison attack, and the independent OF-PTXT proof permits arbitrary epoch-key leakage.
